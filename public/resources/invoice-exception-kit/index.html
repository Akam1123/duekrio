Prepared digital kit · Product preview

Give every invoice blocker a clearer next move.

The B2B Invoice Exception Resolution Kit is a set of editable decision aids, procedures, and message drafts for teams that follow up on overdue business invoices.

Not for sale yet. There is no checkout or payment on this page.

Aging reports identify open invoices. This kit is designed to help a person record what is blocking an invoice, assign an owner, and choose a reviewed next action. It can be used on its own; the Duekrio browser workspace is an optional, free companion.

Inside the prepared version

The current version is packaged as a downloadable ZIP with a 29-page printable PDF, five editable Markdown files, a quick-start file, a file manifest, and an internal-use license.

Decide

8 decision cards

Prompts for common invoice exceptions and the questions to settle before choosing a next step.

Coordinate

Weekly procedure and handoff

A review routine and handoff matrix for naming the blocker, owner, action, and check date.

Write

16 message drafts

Editable starting points for human-reviewed business invoice communication.

Practice

10 fictional cases

Worked examples and a setup guide to help a team adapt the material to its process.

What this kit does not do

  • It is not a subscription, software account, cloud backup, payment processor, automated reminder service, or debt-collection service.
  • It does not verify that a debt is owed, update an accounting ledger, send messages, or promise payment or recovery.
  • It is educational operational material, not legal, tax, accounting, financial, or regulated debt-collection advice.
Keep a person and the source records in control.

Check amounts and payment status in your accounting system. Review recipients, wording, attachments, contracts, and internal approvals before sending anything. The cases in the kit are fictional.
