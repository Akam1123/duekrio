Use terms & product limits

A focused tool for human invoice follow-up.

Read what this free early-access workspace does, what it does not do, and how to use it responsibly.

Last updated September 25, 2026

Purpose and permitted use

Duenara is designed for people managing business-to-business invoices for a business they are authorized to represent. Use it only with records you are permitted to handle and in accordance with your contracts and applicable law. It is not designed for consumer debt collection, operating a collection agency, or processing payments.

Do not enter passwords, payment card details, bank account credentials, government identity numbers, medical records, or other highly sensitive material in an invoice or note.

Your records and decisions

You choose what to import or enter. The current version stores the workspace in this browser on this device; it has no account system, cloud sync, or application server that receives your imported invoice records. Your organization remains responsible for its customer data, including authority to use it, accuracy, access controls, retention, and any notices or rights requests that apply to it. See Privacy & data storage for the data flow and the web host's role.

Check the source ledger.

An imported CSV is a snapshot. Duenara does not verify balances, payment status, customer identity, or whether an invoice is legally due. Confirm these facts in your accounting system and recent correspondence before acting.

Messages and collections

The app prepares draft email text for you to review. It does not send messages, contact customers, collect payments, issue legal notices, or act as a collection agent. You decide whether, when, and to whom to send a message. Check the recipient, facts, tone, and any communication rules that apply to your business before sending it. Do not use a draft to make a claim or threat you cannot support.

Backups and device security

Browser storage can be cleared, corrupted, or unavailable. Keep a current JSON backup if the work matters, and protect the browser profile and any files you export. The live browser workspace is not password-protected or encrypted by Duenara. You may choose a passphrase-encrypted JSON backup for the exported file; the passphrase is not stored or recoverable by Duenara. Readable JSON backups and CSV exports remain unencrypted. Anyone with access to your device, browser profile, or unencrypted exports may be able to read them. Recovery is not guaranteed.

Professional advice and availability

Duenara is a workflow aid, not accounting, tax, financial, or legal advice. It does not replace your accountant, counsel, collection policy, or accounting records. This is a free early-access version; features and availability may change, and no uptime, support response, collection result, or financial outcome is promised.

To the extent applicable law allows, the software and website are provided as available and without warranties. This statement does not limit rights or protections that cannot lawfully be waived. The public source code is separately available under its MIT license.

Feedback

Non-confidential feedback can be shared through the public GitHub issue tracker. Software security vulnerabilities can be reported through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting. This is not a private customer-support or privacy-request channel. Do not post customer names, invoice details, backups, or other private information in public issues.
