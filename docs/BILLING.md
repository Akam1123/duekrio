# Billing and monetization plan — not live

**Reviewed 25 September 2026.** This is an implementation plan, not a live checkout, merchant account, legal opinion, or promise of revenue. Duenara is currently a free, local-first prototype. There is no payment form, subscription, customer account, premium entitlement, or payment processor connected to the site. No marketing or sales campaign has started. Do not accept card details in a Duenara form, email, chat, CSV, or note.

## Commercial offer to validate

Keep the present single-device app and fictional demo free. Its current source is [MIT licensed](../LICENSE), so do not pretend that a paywall around the existing bundle creates an exclusive product. Test a **$29/month single-operator Pro** offer only after a distinct, working premium benefit is shipped and demonstrated. The first candidate is a reliable hosted backup and restore service with authenticated access, encrypted storage, clear recovery behavior, and independently tested data isolation. This is a **product hypothesis**, not a shipped capability or a price commitment. It would add server-side personal-data and security obligations. If research shows that buyers value a different capability more, change the offer before billing anyone.

A **$59/month team plan** is a later hypothesis, contingent on real team accounts, permissions, shared state, audit history, and safe synchronization. The present owner field is only a local text label; it is not a team account. A paid guided setup service is not the recommended initial offer: it needs a human to deliver it, and both candidate merchants restrict services. Charge only for a deliverable that already works, describe exactly what the buyer receives, and display the renewal, cancellation, and refund terms before checkout. No collection-rate or time-savings claim should be used without evidence.

The original [first-customer plan](GO_TO_MARKET.md) remains a validation gate: observe repeat use and ask buyers for a paid commitment at an explicit price. A working checkout alone is not proof of demand or profit.

## Provider choice and eligibility

**Conditional first choice: Paddle as merchant of record (MoR).** [Paddle's published price](https://www.paddle.com/pricing) is 5% + US$0.50 per Checkout transaction, with no setup or monthly fee on its pay-as-you-go plan. Its [supplier-country policy](https://www.paddle.com/help/legal/sanctions/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle) accepts software businesses outside its excluded-country list; Israel is not on that list. This does **not** establish the owner's actual country, guarantee approval, or make Duenara's product category acceptable. Paddle's [acceptable-use policy](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle) welcomes B2B SaaS but excludes regulated financial and payment services, credit advice, and some other categories. Duenara must remain a B2B invoice-workflow tool, not a debt-collection agency, payment service, or financial adviser. Present the real product honestly during underwriting and get the provider's decision before enabling live checkout.

Paddle's [account verification](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/account-verification/what-is-account-verification) requires domain, business (where applicable), and identity checks. Its [domain review](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/account-verification/what-is-domain-verification) requires a live HTTPS site with product description, real pricing and deliverables, accessible terms, refund policy and privacy policy, plus the real company or sole proprietor identity in terms. Its [identity process](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/account-verification/what-is-identity-verification) may require a government ID, proof of address, and a liveness check from the account holder. These cannot truthfully be invented or bypassed by an assistant. Paddle's [payout rules](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/get-paid/when-and-how-do-i-get-paid) currently use monthly payouts with a minimum US$100 threshold; bank and currency costs may apply.

**Fallback to evaluate: Lemon Squeezy MoR.** It [lists Israel for bank payouts](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries), publishes [5% + US$0.50 and no monthly charge](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing), and supports SaaS subscriptions and license keys. However, its [prohibited-products list](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products) explicitly mentions regulated *collections* services and generally excludes services/consulting. Because Duenara handles overdue business invoices, seek written category confirmation before relying on this provider. [Store activation](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/activate-your-store) requires business information, identity verification, and provider review. Its [payout threshold](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/getting-paid) is US$50. The base transaction fee may have additions for some payment methods, international transactions, or payouts.

**Do not assume Stripe is available.** [Stripe's direct merchant-country list](https://stripe.com/global) does not include Israel as of this review. Stripe may be an option if the real legal business is in a supported jurisdiction and passes its own onboarding; a Hebrew interface, timezone, or computer location does not prove the business jurisdiction. Do not create a foreign entity merely to bypass eligibility without independent legal and tax advice.

The merchant of record handles checkout card collection and the customer-facing transaction, including applicable customer sales-tax calculation and remittance under its terms ([Paddle's MoR explanation](https://developer.paddle.com/get-started/how-paddle-works/)). That is **not** personal immunity from lawsuits, product/privacy duties, business registration, or tax on the seller's income. [Paddle's payout statements guidance](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/get-paid/what-statements-will-i-receive) explicitly distinguishes seller income/corporation tax from customer sales tax. Determine actual operator, jurisdiction, banking, and tax position before receiving money.

## Unit economics: scenario, not forecast

For a hypothetical US$29 monthly base price, Paddle's published 5% + US$0.50 fee is US$1.95 per successful transaction. These are simple arithmetic examples **before** other possible fees, currency conversion, refunds, disputes, income tax, hosting beyond free limits, support, or customer acquisition. The amount available to the seller depends on the checkout's tax-inclusive/exclusive setting and actual buyer mix.

| Paying accounts | Monthly gross at US$29 | Base Paddle fees | Remainder before other costs |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | US$290.00 | US$19.50 | US$270.50 |
| 50 | US$1,450.00 | US$97.50 | US$1,352.50 |
| 100 | US$2,900.00 | US$195.00 | US$2,705.00 |

No paying accounts or profit have been verified. A provider with no upfront fee still charges per sale, can hold payouts, and may reject the account or product. A free `*.pages.dev` site URL is not the same thing as owning a `.com` domain.

## Secure implementation boundary

1. **Before any checkout:** the dedicated HTTPS origin [duenara.pages.dev](https://duenara.pages.dev/) is live on Cloudflare Pages Free, but it has **not been approved by a payment provider**. Complete the real operator/contact and brand review, ship a genuine paid capability, publish accurate pricing, renewal/cancellation/refund terms, and update privacy/terms for billing data and the provider. Keep the existing free data export usable. No paid plan is promised until those steps are complete; see [hosting status](HOSTING.md).
2. **Checkout:** use the provider's hosted or overlay checkout on its approved origin. For Paddle, the public [client-side token and price ID](https://developer.paddle.com/paddle-js/about/) may be in the browser; merchant API keys and webhook secrets must never be bundled into Vite assets or committed. The browser must never collect or forward PAN, CVV, or other card data. An approved [default payment-link page](https://developer.paddle.com/build/transactions/default-payment-link/) is required for Paddle live checkout. Serve the checkout script only where needed and revisit the site's Content Security Policy and privacy notice before enabling it.
3. **Identity and entitlement:** first authenticate a buyer and bind one provider customer/subscription to that account. Choose and review the authentication method and recovery process before implementation; the current app has no accounts. A payment-success URL or client callback is display information only. Grant or revoke premium access only from a verified provider event or a server-to-provider check. Define behavior for renewals, cancellations at period end, failed payments, refunds, chargebacks, replay, and temporary provider outages. Give customers a route to access and export their own data after cancellation.
4. **Webhook backend:** a future server endpoint (for example, a separately built Cloudflare Worker or Pages Function) must verify Paddle's `Paddle-Signature` against the **raw** body with a secret held server-side and a time window ([Paddle signature verification](https://developer.paddle.com/webhooks/about/signature-verification/)). The current Pages deployment is static and has no such endpoint. Cap request size and reject unsupported methods. Store event IDs for idempotency, account/subscription state, and an event timestamp or provider readback so duplicated or out-of-order deliveries cannot re-enable a canceled plan. [Paddle documents at-least-once, potentially repeated webhooks](https://developer.paddle.com/webhooks/about/how-webhooks-work/). Redact billing PII from logs, rotate secrets, and test forged, replayed, duplicated, delayed, failed-payment, refund, and cancellation events before live use.
5. **Data separation and encryption:** keep cardholder data entirely with the MoR. If premium cloud backup is built, design tenant authorization and key management first; encrypt transport and stored content, specify who can decrypt, and test recovery and deletion. Browser `localStorage`, CSV exports, and plain JSON backups remain **unencrypted**. The optional passphrase-encrypted JSON backup protects only that downloaded file; losing its passphrase means the file cannot be restored. A cipher added without key recovery, access control, and backup restore tests is not a security solution. A local-only client-side paywall is also bypassable, especially when the current source is public under MIT. Server-side entitlements must protect an actual server-delivered premium service.

There is intentionally no live buy button, real price ID, provider API key, account, or charge in this repository. Build and sandbox-test the integration when the account, approved domain, real product offer, and operator identity exist. Then verify an end-to-end test purchase, renewal, cancellation, failed payment, refund, payout reporting, and the actual live site before accepting real payments. No architecture can guarantee that fraud, security breaches, claims, or chargebacks are impossible.
