**Orvaket is a provisional product name, not a cleared trademark.** The free browser app is live at [orvaket.pages.dev](https://orvaket.pages.dev/); the 26 September release passed direct checks, recorded in [HOSTING.md](HOSTING.md). A Cloudflare subdomain and GitHub repository name confer neither trademark rights nor ownership of a custom domain.

On 26 September 2026, narrow checks of the exact word `Orvaket` and close spellings `Orvacet` and `Orvakat` found no relevant indexed active product use or matching wordmarks in the [official USPTO search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/). The [Israeli open trademark dataset](https://data.gov.il/api/3/action/datastore_search?resource_id=6284d4a9-fdd4-45c9-a1d6-58143c7d8127&q=Orvaket) returned zero exact matches for those queries. These point-in-time checks do **not** establish availability in Israel, the United States, or anywhere else. The WIPO database could not be completed in this session; phonetic variants, unregistered use, other classes, other jurisdictions, and later filings remain open. The [USPTO's clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) explains why a register search alone is insufficient. A qualified market-specific review is appropriate before a paid or larger-scale launch.

## Why earlier names changed

**Duekrio** was the previous public name. The active [Duerio bill-tracking application](https://www.duerio.com/) and its [privacy notice](https://duerio.com/privacy.html) show an existing use adjacent to billing and payments; `Duekrio` differs by one inserted letter. That is a concrete naming risk, not a legal conclusion about infringement or ownership. Earlier exact-word searches for `Duekrio` in the USPTO and Israeli dataset found no match, but exact absence did not resolve similarity or actual-use risk. Its old [Cloudflare site](https://duekrio.pages.dev/) remains for migration, not promotion.

**Duenara** was the first public prototype at [duenara.pages.dev](https://duenara.pages.dev/). A [pending US DENARA application](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn50084123) in finance-adjacent education/software was recorded in the prior review; separate [Denara.ai](https://denara.ai/) and [Denara Group](https://www.denaragroup.com/) sites also use the spelling. Duenara and Denara may sound similar. This was a review flag, not a finding of infringement. The [USPTO explains](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) that sound, appearance, and related services can affect confusion.

**PromiseLedger / Promise Ledger** was an initial working name. [Promise Ledger](https://promiseledger.dev/) is a commercial customer-commitment product, and a separate [public PromiseLedger project](https://github.com/vsrikar08/promise-ledger) tracks commitments. The legacy GitHub Pages path remains technical and migration history, not a promoted product name.

## Release controls

The user authorized a **free-only launch** and **zero-cash promotion** after live verification. The verified release is online; a six-URL IndexNow request returned HTTP 202, which does not prove indexing or users. No social post, direct outreach, or paid campaign is evidenced by this document. Recheck the live title, social image, links, screenshots, privacy/terms, and prepared copy before each new distribution action. Duekrio and Duenara now show migration guidance and remain available so existing users can export and restore: browser storage belongs to each origin, so changing from `duenara.pages.dev` or `duekrio.pages.dev` to `orvaket.pages.dev` does not carry data automatically. Preserve storage keys and backup format during the visible rename. Do not present Orvaket as trademark-cleared, and do not market the inactive paid kit as a current offer.
