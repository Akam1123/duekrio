# Brand check before promotion

**Duenara is a provisional product name, not a cleared trademark.** A preliminary exact-name web search on 25 September 2026 did not find a related software product called Duenara. That narrow result does not prove availability, ownership, registration eligibility, or freedom to use the name. Similar spellings and sounds, unindexed use, and national trademark records still matter. The address [duenara.pages.dev](https://duenara.pages.dev/) is live, but control of a Pages subdomain does not establish trademark rights or ownership of a custom domain.

The previous working name, **PromiseLedger / Promise Ledger**, has material collision risk. [Promise Ledger](https://promiseledger.dev/) is a live commercial customer-commitment product, with its own [terms](https://promiseledger.dev/terms) and [privacy policy](https://promiseledger.dev/privacy). A separate [public PromiseLedger project](https://github.com/vsrikar08/promise-ledger) also tracks customer commitments. This is evidence of existing use and possible customer confusion, not a determination of trademark ownership or infringement. Keep the old GitHub path as a legacy data-migration address; do not promote it as the brand.

Before actively promoting or charging for Duenara, check identical and similar marks, including phonetic variants and related finance, collections, and software services, in the [USPTO trademark search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search), [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database), and relevant national registers such as [Israel's free self-search](https://www.gov.il/he/service/search_israeli_trademarks_database). Check domain records through [ICANN lookup](https://lookup.icann.org/) and current web/product use. The [USPTO's clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) says internet use and marks without federal registration can matter. A professional clearance review may still be appropriate before paid launch in a chosen market.

**Marketing remains on hold.** No external promotion, posts, outreach, or advertising are authorized. The current host, canonical URL, privacy disclosure, and key browser flows were checked at deployment; recheck site title, social metadata, links, screenshots, legal pages, and all prepared copy before any future distribution. Preserve the existing browser storage key during a visual rename so current visitors do not lose data; changing hostnames still changes the browser storage origin, so provide backup and restore guidance.
