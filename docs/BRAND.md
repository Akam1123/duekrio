# Brand check before promotion

**Duekrio is a provisional product name, not a cleared trademark.** A preliminary exact-name web search on 25 September 2026 found no indexed B2B finance or software product using the exact word “Duekrio.” On 26 September, [official USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/) wordmark queries across all statuses and classes returned no results for `DUEKRIO`, `DUKRIO`, or `DUCRIO`; `KRIO` produced records mostly in unrelated categories. The [Israeli official open trademark dataset](https://data.gov.il/api/3/action/datastore_search?resource_id=6284d4a9-fdd4-45c9-a1d6-58143c7d8127&q=Duekrio), last updated 18 September 2026, returned no results for the exact name or the checked variants `Due Krio`, `Dukrio`, `Ducrio`, `Krio`, and `דוקריו`. These narrow checks do not establish trademark availability, ownership, or freedom to use the name. Broader similarity, common-law use, relevant countries, and WIPO searches remain incomplete. A Cloudflare Pages project name or subdomain does not confer brand rights or ownership of a custom domain.

The **Krio** component is in use. A search-indexed result for [krio.tr](https://krio.tr/) described a B2B operations platform with financial reconciliation, while a fresh live visit showed different “Krio Plan” branding without enough content to establish its current offering. Its present services and legal rights have not been established by this check. Whether buyers would associate either use with Duekrio is unknown; review similar marks and actual market use before promotion or charging. Unregistered uses, sound-alike words, other countries, and later filings also matter. No custom-domain purchase or trademark filing has been made for Duekrio in this project.

## Why the earlier names changed

**Duenara** was used for the initial public prototype at [duenara.pages.dev](https://duenara.pages.dev/). The [USPTO record for DENARA](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn50084123), checked 25 September 2026, showed a live pending US standard-character application, serial **50084123**, filed 1 September 2026, for financial-literacy education and related educational software services in classes 041 and 042. Separate public sites use the DENARA spelling: [Denara.ai](https://denara.ai/) describes AI-agent payment software, and [Denara Group](https://www.denaragroup.com/) describes a WealthTech group. The search did not establish that either business owns that US application or its legal status. Duenara and Denara differ in spelling but may sound similar and share finance/software audiences. This is a review flag, not a finding of infringement or a prediction about the application. The [USPTO explains](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) that sound, appearance, and related services can affect confusion.

The original working name **PromiseLedger / Promise Ledger** also has material collision risk. [Promise Ledger](https://promiseledger.dev/) is a live commercial customer-commitment product, and a separate [public PromiseLedger project](https://github.com/vsrikar08/promise-ledger) tracks customer commitments. The GitHub repository and its old Pages path remain as technical history and data-migration access, not the promoted product name.
