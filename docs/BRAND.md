# Brand check before promotion

**Duekrio is a provisional product name, not a cleared trademark.** A preliminary exact-name web search on 25 September 2026 found no indexed B2B finance or software product using the exact word “Duekrio.” An exact word-name query in the Israeli trademark register returned no result. Those narrow checks do not establish trademark availability, ownership, or freedom to use the name. Broad similarity searches in the US and WIPO interfaces remain incomplete. A Cloudflare Pages project name or subdomain does not confer brand rights or ownership of a custom domain.

The **Krio** component is in use. A search-indexed result for [krio.tr](https://krio.tr/) described a B2B operations platform with financial reconciliation, while a fresh visit to its `www` version showed a scheduling site. Its current offering and legal rights have not been established by this check. Whether buyers would associate either use with Duekrio is unknown; review similar marks and actual market use before promotion or charging. Unregistered uses, sound-alike words, other countries, and later filings also matter. No custom-domain purchase or trademark filing has been made for Duekrio in this project.

## Why the earlier names changed

**Duenara** was used for the initial public prototype at [duenara.pages.dev](https://duenara.pages.dev/). The [USPTO record for DENARA](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn50084123), checked 25 September 2026, showed a live pending US standard-character application, serial **50084123**, filed 1 September 2026, for financial-literacy education and related educational software services in classes 041 and 042. Separate public sites use the DENARA spelling: [Denara.ai](https://denara.ai/) describes AI-agent payment software, and [Denara Group](https://www.denaragroup.com/) describes a WealthTech group. The search did not establish that either business owns that US application or its legal status. Duenara and Denara differ in spelling but may sound similar and share finance/software audiences. This is a review flag, not a finding of infringement or a prediction about the application. The [USPTO explains](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) that sound, appearance, and related services can affect confusion.

The original working name **PromiseLedger / Promise Ledger** also has material collision risk. [Promise Ledger](https://promiseledger.dev/) is a live commercial customer-commitment product, and a separate [public PromiseLedger project](https://github.com/vsrikar08/promise-ledger) tracks customer commitments. The GitHub repository and its old Pages path remain as technical history and data-migration access, not the promoted product name.

## Before promotion or paid operation

Complete a market-specific search of identical and similar marks, sound-alike variants, and related finance, collections, and software services in the [USPTO trademark search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search), [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database), and relevant national registers such as [Israel's free self-search](https://www.gov.il/he/service/search_israeli_trademarks_database). The broad USPTO search interface was unavailable during this preliminary review, and WIPO required a CAPTCHA; neither search was completed. Check current web/product use and domain records through [ICANN lookup](https://lookup.icann.org/). The [USPTO's clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) notes that internet use and unregistered marks can matter. A qualified review is appropriate before a paid launch in a chosen market.
