The assistant will copy this table into a private working document each Friday. Record `unknown` when the app or channel does not expose a metric; do not enter zero for unknown. Include dated public links and submission receipts, and never populate the log with synthetic campaign results. **Qualified conversation:** A person who actually handles AR follow-up at a small B2B service firm describes a recent specific overdue-invoice exception, current tool, and next-action process. A comment, like, generic compliment, or unrelated signup does not count.

**Sample demo:** The person says or shows that they opened and used the fictional board. Public clicks alone are not observable without analytics.

**Pilot start:** A qualified firm agrees to use its own authorized export on its own device for a defined two-week period and completes first import plus five invoice triage actions. Keep customer details out of the shared log.

**Second cycle:** The same firm returns on a later weekly AR review and shows that it updated actions or reconciled a new export. A tab refresh in the same session does not count.

**Paid commitment:** In a later paid phase only, an explicit yes to a defined, currently deliverable offer at a stated price, ideally a transaction. A hypothetical “I might pay” does not count. The free launch has no paid offer or checkout.

**Recovery and time claims:** Record cash only when confirmed in the buyer's accounting ledger, with permission. Do not attribute all paid invoices to the product. Record time savings as self-reported unless timed against a baseline.

Use ranges rather than exact balances. Keep a separate private contact list if follow-up is requested; do not commit contacts, email addresses, or customer invoice data to GitHub. Stop outreach when someone declines.

## Four-week decision

- **Product problem:** Two or more qualified users report the same import failure, mistaken financial state, or loss-of-work risk. Fix and retest before increasing distribution.
- **Message problem:** Qualified people understand the problem but cannot tell what the product does or what data it keeps. Revise the landing copy and 45-second demo, then test with fresh viewers.
- **Audience problem:** After enough real conversations, the target firms already solve the exception workflow easily in their existing tools. Narrow to a different service niche or stop this wedge.
- **Promising free-use signal:** Multiple firms independently repeat the weekly workflow and ask to continue. Continue the same channel and measure repeatability. A later, separately enabled paid offer is needed to test revenue.

These are operating judgments, not statistical significance or proof of product-market fit. Compare with the broader thresholds in [the first-customer plan](../GO_TO_MARKET.md).
