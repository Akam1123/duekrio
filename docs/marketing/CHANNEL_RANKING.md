# Duenara: where to promote, and how to test it

Research checked 25 September 2026. **Planning only: no marketing has been published or sent.** The user asked to finish the site, payments, and safety work before promotion. This is a zero-cash plan for reaching operators at small US B2B service firms who personally handle overdue invoice exceptions. Rankings are hypotheses about buyer fit, not measured conversion rates or promises of reach.

## Readiness gate

Before any external distribution, verify the final live URL, accurate product and price claims, a private support/contact route, a usable fictional demo, and an authorized identity for any account used to post or reply. Payment processing, if advertised, must be live and testable. Do not invite firms to upload real AR data to an unverified host or request raw customer exports by email. If these gates fail, improve the owned site and product; record distribution as **not started**.

## Ranked zero-cost channels

| 1 | **Warm adviser introductions:** independent bookkeepers or fractional finance operators who already help service firms review AR. | The adviser can identify an actual weekly workflow and introduce a buyer who has the problem. This is a buyer-proximity hypothesis, not a proven partnership channel. | From already authorized contacts or inbound requests, seek 5 adviser critiques of a fictional sample. | 2 | **One practical search guide on the owned site:** owners and bookkeepers searching for an AR aging follow-up process. | Matches the task a buyer is trying to do; the site can explain the product's exact scope. Search is slow and has no guaranteed rankings. | Publish one original guide showing a fictional aging row, missing-PO blocker, owner, next step, review date, and ledger reconciliation. Link to the sample board. Check indexability, canonical URL, sitemap, mobile readability, and source accuracy. Improve this one page based on real queries before making another. | Medium production effort: one strong article and a real screenshot; $0 cash. | Indexed status and relevant non-branded impressions/clicks in Search Console **only if the property is verified**; qualified inbound questions and pilot starts. | 4 | **Question-led communities:** an existing legitimate account in a forum where a real AR workflow question appears. | A detailed answer can test whether the problem resonates, but communities are often hostile to pitches and have uneven buyer fit. | Answer up to five distinct, relevant questions with a standalone workflow; disclose the maker relationship. Add a site link only when that specific community and thread allow it. Track qualified AR operators separately from general votes. | Concentrated launch/reply effort; $0 cash. | Qualified buyer conversations and pilots, not upvotes. | Product Hunt requires a personal account and new accounts may need a waiting period. Do not buy votes or create a new account solely to force a launch without checking account terms and access. |

**Paid ads:** Google/LinkedIn/Reddit ads are outside a zero-cash budget and should not run. No ad account, card, or campaign is needed for this plan. Broad startup directories, generic social blasts, and accounting app marketplaces are not the first wedge. In particular, a Xero App Store listing requires an actual certified integration and current platform terms; a CSV-only browser app does not qualify merely because it accepts Xero exports.

## How to choose the winner

Run the channels in order **only where an authorized account or relationship already exists**. The owned guide can be prepared without an external posting identity once the site is ready. For each channel, log dated work, public URL or permitted introduction source, time spent, qualified conversations, pilot starts, second-cycle use, and paid commitments. Record `unknown` when a platform does not expose a metric. At four weeks, compare **qualified conversations and repeated pilots per unit of effort**, not raw impressions. If few people respond, inspect actual query/reply evidence before changing the product or writing more content. A four-week SEO result alone is too early to declare failure.

No revenue forecast follows from this ranking. A channel becomes promising only after real buyers repeatedly use the workflow and some accept a currently deliverable stated-price offer. ## Source checks

- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) and [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) support a useful, original guide instead of scaled AI pages. [Search Console performance documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en) defines available search metrics; site ownership must be verified for access.
- [LinkedIn's free Company Page and analytics description](https://www.business.linkedin.com/advertise/linkedin-pages) supports organic posting as a no-ad-spend option; [LinkedIn's automated-activity policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1340567/automated-activity-on-linkedin?lang=en) sets the automation boundary.
- [Reddit's spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam) and [QuickBooks Community guidelines](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/community/quickbooks-news-64/community-guidelines-164537) determine whether a community contribution may include promotion.
- [Product Hunt posting requirements](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product) and [platform fit guidance](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works) explain the personal-account and usable-product requirements.
- [Xero App Store partner requirements](https://developer.xero.com/documentation/xero-app-store/app-partner-guides/building-and-growing-your-app/) and [Xero's current tiers](https://developer.xero.com/pricing) show why marketplace listing is a later integration project.
- [FTC CAN-SPAM guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) says US commercial-email requirements also apply to B2B and are not limited to bulk sends.
