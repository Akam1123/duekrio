## Product and audience

**Turn an accounts receivable aging CSV into a clear next-action list for invoices that standard reminders have not resolved.** Orvaket records the blocker, owner label, next step, and payment promise beside each invoice, then drafts a follow-up for a person to check and send. It is a local-first browser app: no account, cloud sync, automatic sending, accounting API, or payment checkout. Data is in that browser on that device; users need an export backup and must verify balances and paid status in their accounting system.

The first audience hypothesis is an owner, operations lead, or bookkeeper at a small US B2B service firm doing a weekly AR review in a spreadsheet and inbox. The current product is in English, with USD and US-style dates. [QuickBooks reminder guidance](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/invoicing/send-invoice-reminders-automatically-manually/L84cQjpxo_US_en_US) and [Xero's AR description](https://www.xero.com/us/accounting-software/accounts-receivable/) illustrate existing reminder and aging features; Orvaket's testable distinction is the human context of invoice exceptions. A fictional sample, not a recovery promise, is the first invitation.

Search Console impressions/clicks are available only for a property verified in an accessible account ([Google's setup guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-console-start)). ## Copy and privacy rules

- Use the exact shipped features and live URL on publication day. Say **free today**; do not imply a forever pricing commitment or live paid offer.
- Use fictional or explicitly authorized redacted invoice data. Never send a buyer's raw AR export or correspondence to an AI tool, public issue, or marketing channel.
- Disclose the maker connection. No fabricated testimonials, customer count, savings, collection rate, security certification, or endorsement.
- Stop unsolicited contact after no reply or an opt-out; follow each platform's rules and applicable law. Do not make the owner complete campaign tasks or spend money.
