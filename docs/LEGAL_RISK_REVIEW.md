# Legal and policy risk review — Duenara early access

**Review date:** September 25, 2026. **Scope:** the public, free, browser-only early-access app and its current repository. This is a practical risk inventory, not a legal opinion or a guarantee against claims. The operator's identity, place of establishment, intended customer countries, and future revenue model are not established in this repository. Those facts change which rules apply.

The owner's instruction is to **hold promotion**. This review does not authorize search submissions, outreach, advertising, or social posts.

## Priority 0: Resolve before treating this as an operating business

1. **Hosting terms.** The current site is on GitHub Pages. GitHub [says Pages may not be used as free hosting to run an online business or provide commercial SaaS](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits). The free prototype is accessible today, but a product meant to become a business should move to a host whose current terms fit its use. A Cloudflare Pages root-host package is prepared, not yet deployed; see [HOSTING.md](HOSTING.md). Verify the replacement host's acceptable-use and free-plan terms before deployment. After a move, verify the privacy page's host/log disclosure, every canonical and social URL, app links, and the public documentation; keep the previous site available for data migration.
2. **Accountable operator and private contact.** The [privacy page](../public/privacy/index.html) describes local storage, but the site does not identify a legal operator or provide a private route for privacy and security reports. GitHub Issues are public and require an account. Do not invent a company, postal address, privacy email, or data protection officer. Once the real operator and applicable jurisdictions are known, publish appropriate identity/contact and rights-request information. The [European Commission's notice guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/obligations_en) and the [Israeli Privacy Protection Authority's guidance](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/duty_to_notify/he/notify13.pdf) explain requirements where their laws apply. Until there is a safe private channel and a reviewed notice, discourage importing confidential customer records.
3. **Brand clearance.** The former working name “PromiseLedger” collides with a [live commercial Promise Ledger product](https://promiseledger.dev/) and a separate [public project](https://github.com/vsrikar08/promise-ledger) in related customer-commitment workflows. The visible product is being renamed **Duenara** to reduce confusion. A preliminary exact-name web search is not trademark clearance, and `duenara.pages.dev` is unreserved. Before promotion or paid operation, search similar marks and actual use in the intended markets; see [BRAND.md](BRAND.md) and the [USPTO's comprehensive clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks). Existing use does not itself prove infringement, and a new name does not guarantee freedom from claims.
4. **Security and incident readiness.** Local-only data reduces server exposure, but a compromised release, browser profile, or plain JSON backup could expose invoice information. Maintain dependency and source review, protected deployment credentials, backups of the code/site, and a procedure to contain a compromised release, notify affected parties where required, and document what happened. The [FTC's small-business guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business) recommends minimizing and protecting retained information and planning ahead; the [Israeli authority has a reporting process for qualifying serious incidents](https://www.gov.il/he/service/report-of-data-breach). Whether any reporting law applies depends on the operator, data, and incident.

## Priority 1: Product and public-site controls

5. **Accurate product terms.** The public [use-terms page](../public/terms/index.html) now explains that this is a B2B workflow aid, not a payment service, collection agency, or professional adviser; drafts require human review and no collection outcome is guaranteed. Keep statements consistent with the product after each release. Do not assume a disclaimer removes statutory duties or protects against a misleading claim. The [FTC requires objective support for advertising claims](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business).
6. **Privacy roles and storage.** The app currently has no analytics, account system, or application server receiving imported records. It stores invoice records in `localStorage` and exports plain files; the hosting provider may process visitor request data. The host disclosure and retention statements must match the actual provider after migration. Explain how a user clears local data and that the business entering customer information controls its own records and notices. Avoid adding third-party analytics or pixels without a new privacy review. Functional storage may be exempt from prior consent only when strictly necessary for the user-requested service; the [ICO explains this exception](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/). Do not add a cosmetic consent banner that misstates what occurs.
7. **Access and accessibility.** Automated scans cannot establish full accessibility. Test keyboard navigation, focus order and visibility, screen-reader names and announcements, zoom/reflow, CSV preview, dialogs, backup/recovery, and all static pages. Fix observed barriers and publish only an accurate accessibility statement and private reporting route. Obligations depend on where and by whom the service is offered; relevant guidance includes [US DOJ's web guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/) and [Israeli internet-service accessibility regulations](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/accommodating_service_providing_rules/he/sitedocs_service_acessibility_regulations.pdf). Do not assume a small-business exemption without checking eligibility.
8. **Debt and message scope.** Keep use restricted to business invoices and make users verify the source ledger, recipients, balance, and prior correspondence before sending. US federal consumer-debt rules do not generally cover business debt, but that does not displace other applicable laws or contracts; see the [FTC's debt-collection FAQ](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/debt-collection-faqs). The product must not imply that generated drafts are legally sufficient notices or automatically sent.

## Conditional rules to reassess as the business changes

- **EU/UK privacy:** A site is not subject to GDPR solely because it can be opened from Europe; a non-EU operator targeting people in the EU or monitoring their behavior may be covered, even for free services. See the [European Commission's territorial-scope explanation](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/reform/rules-business-and-organisations/application-regulation/who-does-data-protection-law-apply_en). Reassess lawful basis, notices, data-subject rights, international transfers, and vendor relationships if targeting changes.
- **Israeli privacy:** If the real operator is in Israel and processes personal data, assess the amended Privacy Protection Law and its security and notice obligations. The [Israeli authority says database-registration exemptions do not erase other duties](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/tikun13_qa?chapterIndex=6). A timezone or local file path is not proof of the operator's legal location.
- **California privacy:** The CCPA depends in part on business and volume thresholds; no evidence here establishes that it applies today. Reassess if business scale, data sharing, or targeting changes ([California DOJ](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa)).
- **Taxes and business registration if monetized:** Pricing, payment collection, invoices to customers, and the operator's actual location determine registration, income-tax, VAT/sales-tax, and recordkeeping duties. Do not turn on paid plans or promise tax treatment based on this review. If operating in Israel, start with the [Tax Authority's business registration guidance](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-open-exempt-dealer-via-internet); if operating in the US, see the [IRS starting-a-business guide](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/starting-a-business). Cross-border sales may add rules, such as the [EU VAT place-of-taxation framework](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/where-tax_en). Get jurisdiction-specific tax advice before billing.
- **Marketing remains paused:** The drafts in `docs/marketing/` are preparation only. No email, social posts, search submissions, or ads should be sent until the owner explicitly starts promotion. Before any later campaign, verify sender identity, audience permissions, truthful product claims, opt-out handling, and local channel rules. Relevant official sources include the [FTC's CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) and [Israeli government guidance on advertising messages](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/cpfta_dncapi/he/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A%20%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D.pdf).

## Remaining unknowns

No page or checklist can guarantee legal immunity or compliance in every country. A qualified adviser needs the real operator's identity and establishment, actual target markets, data flows after hosting migration, pricing plans, and any customer contracts to give a legal opinion. Until then, preserve the current truthful free-prototype positioning, avoid unsupported savings or recovery claims, and keep promotion on hold.
