# Legal and policy risk review — Duekrio early access

**Review date:** September 25, 2026. **Scope:** the public, free, browser-only early-access app and its current repository. This is a practical risk inventory, not a legal opinion or a guarantee against claims. The operator's identity, place of establishment, intended customer countries, and future revenue model are not established in this repository. Those facts change which rules apply.

The owner's instruction is to **hold promotion**. This review does not authorize search submissions, outreach, advertising, or social posts.

## Priority 0: Resolve before treating this as an operating business

1. **Hosting terms and release control.** The Duekrio release is live at [duekrio.pages.dev](https://duekrio.pages.dev/) on Cloudflare Pages Free, with public source at [Akam1123/duekrio](https://github.com/Akam1123/duekrio); see the point-in-time checks in [HOSTING.md](HOSTING.md). The prior [Duenara Cloudflare address](https://duenara.pages.dev/) and former GitHub Pages address must remain accessible so visitors can export browser data for migration. GitHub [says Pages may not be used as free hosting to run an online business or provide commercial SaaS](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits). Cloudflare projects here use direct upload, not automatic GitHub integration, so each release needs a separately built, uploaded, and verified package. Continue to review Cloudflare's terms, usage limits, and each release's privacy and link accuracy. Free hosting does not establish a legal business or remove operator duties.
2. **Accountable operator and private contact.** The [privacy page](../public/privacy/index.html) describes local storage, but the site does not identify a legal operator or provide a private route for customer privacy requests. GitHub Issues are public and require an account. GitHub [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Akam1123/duekrio/security/advisories/new) is enabled on the new repository for software security findings. Neither route fills the privacy-contact gap. Do not invent a company, postal address, privacy email, or data protection officer. Once the real operator and applicable jurisdictions are known, publish appropriate identity/contact and rights-request information. The [European Commission's notice guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/obligations_en) and the [Israeli Privacy Protection Authority's guidance](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/duty_to_notify/he/notify13.pdf) explain requirements where their laws apply. Until there is a safe private privacy channel and a reviewed notice, discourage importing confidential customer records.
3. **Brand clearance.** The original “PromiseLedger” name has a live commercial [Promise Ledger product](https://promiseledger.dev/) and a separate [public project](https://github.com/vsrikar08/promise-ledger) in related workflows. Duenara was then used publicly, but the pending US **DENARA** application (serial 50084123) and existing finance/software uses created a similarity review flag. **Duekrio** is the new provisional name; a narrow exact-name check was empty, while the Krio component appears in indexed B2B finance/operations copy for [krio.tr](https://krio.tr/) even though the current `www` site presents a different scheduling service. Broad USPTO and WIPO similarity searches remain incomplete. The `duekrio.pages.dev` project does not confer brand rights. Before promotion or paid operation, review similar marks and actual use in the intended markets; see [BRAND.md](BRAND.md) and the [USPTO's comprehensive clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks).
4. **Security and incident readiness.** Local-only data reduces server exposure, but a compromised release, browser profile, or plain JSON backup could expose invoice information. Maintain dependency and source review, protected deployment credentials, and backups of the code/site. Follow the concrete [incident response checklist](INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md) to contain a compromised release, assess notice duties, and document what happened. The [FTC's small-business guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business) recommends minimizing and protecting retained information and planning ahead; the [Israeli authority has a reporting process for qualifying serious incidents](https://www.gov.il/he/service/report-of-data-breach). Whether any reporting law applies depends on the operator, data, and incident.

## Priority 1: Product and public-site controls

5. **Accurate product terms.** The public [use-terms page](../public/terms/index.html) now explains that this is a B2B workflow aid, not a payment service, collection agency, or professional adviser; drafts require human review and no collection outcome is guaranteed. Keep statements consistent with the product after each release. Do not assume a disclaimer removes statutory duties or protects against a misleading claim. The [FTC requires objective support for advertising claims](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business).
6. **Privacy roles and storage.** The app has no analytics, account system, or application server receiving imported records. By default it stores readable invoice records in browser `localStorage`. Optional local workspace encryption protects future saves, but an older readable copy remains until a verified backup and explicit removal; decrypted data is available while the tab is unlocked and may be exposed by a compromised origin or extension. CSV and plain JSON exports remain readable, and optional encrypted JSON backups use a separate passphrase. Cloudflare may process visitor request information to serve the site. A private privacy-request route and accountable operator are still missing. Explain how a user clears local data and that the business entering customer information controls its own records and notices. Avoid adding third-party analytics or pixels without a new privacy review. Functional storage may be exempt from prior consent only when strictly necessary for the user-requested service; the [ICO explains this exception](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/). Do not add a cosmetic consent banner that misstates what occurs.
7. **Access and accessibility.** Automated scans cannot establish full accessibility. Test keyboard navigation, focus order and visibility, screen-reader names and announcements, zoom/reflow, CSV preview, dialogs, backup/recovery, and all static pages. Fix observed barriers and publish only an accurate accessibility statement and private reporting route. Obligations depend on where and by whom the service is offered; relevant guidance includes [US DOJ's web guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/) and [Israeli internet-service accessibility regulations](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/accommodating_service_providing_rules/he/sitedocs_service_acessibility_regulations.pdf). Do not assume a small-business exemption without checking eligibility.
8. **Debt and message scope.** Keep use restricted to business invoices and make users verify the source ledger, recipients, balance, and prior correspondence before sending. US federal consumer-debt rules do not generally cover business debt, but that does not displace other applicable laws or contracts; see the [FTC's debt-collection FAQ](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/debt-collection-faqs). The product must not imply that generated drafts are legally sufficient notices or automatically sent.

## Conditional rules to reassess as the business changes

- **EU/UK privacy:** A site is not subject to GDPR solely because it can be opened from Europe; a non-EU operator targeting people in the EU or monitoring their behavior may be covered, even for free services. See the [European Commission's territorial-scope explanation](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/reform/rules-business-and-organisations/application-regulation/who-does-data-protection-law-apply_en). Reassess lawful basis, notices, data-subject rights, international transfers, and vendor relationships if targeting changes.
- **Israeli privacy:** If the real operator is in Israel and processes personal data, assess the amended Privacy Protection Law and its security and notice obligations. The [Israeli authority says database-registration exemptions do not erase other duties](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/tikun13_qa?chapterIndex=6). A timezone or local file path is not proof of the operator's legal location.
- **California privacy:** The CCPA depends in part on business and volume thresholds; no evidence here establishes that it applies today. Reassess if business scale, data sharing, or targeting changes ([California DOJ](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa)).
- **Taxes and business registration if monetized:** Pricing, payment collection, invoices to customers, and the operator's actual location determine registration, income-tax, VAT/sales-tax, and recordkeeping duties. The [billing plan](BILLING.md) is conditional and no payment provider or premium entitlement is live. Before collecting money, the real operator must complete merchant identity and payout verification; the site must publish accurate pricing, renewal, cancellation, refund, privacy, and operator terms, and a genuine paid benefit must work. Do not turn on paid plans or promise tax treatment based on this review. If operating in Israel, start with the [Tax Authority's business registration guidance](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-open-exempt-dealer-via-internet); if operating in the US, see the [IRS starting-a-business guide](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/starting-a-business). Cross-border sales may add rules, such as the [EU VAT place-of-taxation framework](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/where-tax_en). Get jurisdiction-specific tax advice before billing.
- **Marketing remains paused:** The drafts in `docs/marketing/` are preparation only. No email, social posts, search submissions, or ads should be sent until the owner explicitly starts promotion. Before any later campaign, verify sender identity, audience permissions, truthful product claims, opt-out handling, and local channel rules. Relevant official sources include the [FTC's CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) and [Israeli government guidance on advertising messages](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/cpfta_dncapi/he/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A%20%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D.pdf).

## Remaining unknowns

No page or checklist can guarantee legal immunity or compliance in every country. A qualified adviser needs the real operator's identity and establishment, actual target markets, the Cloudflare hosting and browser-storage data flows, pricing plans, and any customer contracts to give a legal opinion. Until then, preserve the current truthful free-prototype positioning, avoid unsupported savings or recovery claims, and keep promotion on hold.
